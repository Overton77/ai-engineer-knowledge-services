# Research and evidence ledger

Status: **REFERENCE supporting a PROPOSED architecture.** [Entrypoint](README.md).

Search covered citation/claim verification, recent deep-research diagnostics, scientific lineage, signed provenance, native-media mappings, retractions, agent injection and product workflows. Followed primary specifications, papers, official documentation and author repositories. This is targeted architecture research, not a systematic review claiming exhaustive recall. Search snippets were leads; the entries below identify the depth actually inspected. No source was run as a benchmark, no vendor account was tested, and no external content was admitted into the system's evidence store.

Strength labels: **S** normative or official specification (strong for prescribed semantics, not implementation); **D** official implementation/product documentation (supports documented capability, not independently measured performance); **P** primary research with methods/results (limited to its task and evaluation); **A** abstract/metadata inspection only (discovery-level evidence). `Live` means an unpinned documentation page, not proof of latest released software. Unknown publication dates are explicit. All rows carry the common access date above; exact versions are listed where inspected. The browsing tool's crawl timestamp is not a publication date.

## Disconfirming findings and decision consequences

- “Add citations” is not a differentiator: R21–R23 document production-facing citation/audit functionality.
- “Invent a provenance graph” is not novel: R11/R18–R20/R31 provide established designs; R08 explicitly overlaps with agent guardrails and invalidation.
- “Always use the largest judge” is not supported: R06 motivates cost/error-direction evaluation.
- “More judges or sources means independent truth” is unsupported: model-family diversity does not establish source-origin independence; copied chains require inspection. The proposal's independence policy is a conservative engineering choice, not an empirically proven estimator.
- “A valid signature proves reality” confuses the semantics of R13–R17; build authenticity and media credentials answer narrower questions.
- “A published benchmark solves production verification” is false scope transfer: benchmark domain, judge and label limitations remain; R05 explicitly excludes tabular citations.
- “Complete lineage can always reach an objective ground truth” is unrealistic: unavailable raw data, ambiguous perception, rights and deletion terminate inspectability. Explicit frontiers are essential.

The resulting differentiation hypothesis is integrated, measured **policy-bound claim reuse and repair across versions**, not any individual borrowed technique. Test this against representative user workflows and actual competing products before making an external uniqueness claim.

## Research limitations and next reads

No full systematic-literature search, competitor procurement/demo, or independent reproduction was performed. Abstract-only rows motivate evaluation/design options and are not used to claim comparative performance. A production standards profile needs pinned full specification revisions and a conformance matrix. Product pages may change after this cutoff. Full source bytes were not archived or cryptographically sealed during this research; browser access is not equivalent to the proposed system's custody chain.

The most useful next deep reviews, after accepting the direction, are: a complete DSSE/in-toto predicate profile; source-family adjudication datasets; rights-cleared video/audio gold; an audit of current report-source dependency traversal coverage; and a hands-on comparison of multi-hop correction workflows. These are implementation/research follow-ups, not prerequisites for reading this proposal.
