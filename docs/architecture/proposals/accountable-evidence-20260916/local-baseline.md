## Scope corrections to the initial brief

The 12/11/14 counts, exact-quote compilation, declared atomicity, first-edge legacy report aggregation and native-media distinction agree with source. They must not obscure the newer `ReportAssessmentService`, canonical report structures, representation eligibility checks or durable recovery closure. Consequently the proposal describes **extension and unified operational proof**, not replacement of absent foundations.

The older admission document says full report/provenance evolution and durable cases are later work. Current report/recovery implementations establish that portions now exist. This research does not edit that existing document during concurrent work; the discrepancy is recorded here. Likewise the verification guide's dependency-pin statement is a dated statement, not proof of the currently deployed schema. Canonical migrations inspected extend beyond that guide's listed head.

“Missing” in the proposal means not provided by the inspected specific surface (for example raw-video executor capture), or a proposed contract not yet established here. “Unknown” is used for system-wide deployment, complete reverse correction coverage, competitor internals, human-gold quality, and workload performance. No universal absence claim is inferred from a search miss.
